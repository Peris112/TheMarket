Comprehensive Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2, 2025

1. Introduction and Scope

Your privacy is a paramount concern for The Market. This comprehensive Privacy Policy governs the manner in which BizConnect collects, utilizes, maintains, and discloses information collected from users of the BizConnect website and any associated mobile applications. This policy applies fully to your access and use of our platform. By accessing or using the Site, you signify your full acknowledgment and acceptance of the terms outlined in this policy.

2. Detailed Information Gathering Practices

We are dedicated to limiting the data we collect to only what is necessary to effectively operate the marketplace, facilitate secure transactions, and enhance your user experience.

A. Personally Identifiable Information (Personal Data)

This category encompasses data that can be used to specifically identify or contact you. We solicit this information at various key interaction points:

  • Registration and Identity Data: Full legal name, unique username, secured password hashes, and user preferences stored within your profile.
  • Transactional and Contact Data: Email address, primary telephone number, billing address, and mandatory shipping addresses required for product delivery and legal invoicing.
  • Financial Data: Tokenized data, payment methods used, and records of payments processed through our third-party compliance partners (we do not store raw card numbers on our servers).
  • Communication Records: Detailed logs and content of messages exchanged between buyers and sellers, stored to ensure compliance, dispute resolution, and security.

B. Non-Personal and Technical Information

This data is gathered passively and automatically whenever you interact with the Site, aiding us in performance monitoring and security:

  • Technical Identifiers: Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type and version, operating system, and hardware model.
  • Usage and Navigational Data: Clickstream data, pages viewed, time spent, referral sources, and interactions with various features.

3. Comprehensive Data Utilization Rationale

The information we gather is systematically processed and used for several core business and service maintenance objectives:

  • Core Service Delivery To establish and maintain your user account, execute and manage all sales and purchase transactions, and ensure the prompt delivery of goods or services.
  • Security, Audit, and Compliance Data is used for ongoing security audits, the proactive detection and prevention of fraudulent activities, and enforcement of our Terms of Use across the community.
  • Platform Refinement and Innovation We analyze Usage and Technical Data to diagnose problems, optimize loading times, and develop new features that enhance the marketplace experience.
  • Customer Support and Communication To respond efficiently to your support inquiries, manage complaints, and send necessary administrative notices, service updates, or security alerts, including notifications related to your Inbox.

4. Policy on Information Disclosure and Third-Party Sharing

We maintain a strict policy against the sale, trade, or renting of your Personal Data to external marketing parties. Disclosure is limited strictly to circumstances critical for service operation or mandated by law:

  • Transactional Necessity: Contact and shipping information is exchanged between the Buyer and Seller to facilitate logistics and post-sale support. This sharing is fundamental to a functioning marketplace.
  • Trusted Service Vendors: We engage external, verified companies (payment processors, hosts) who are granted access only to the minimum Personal Data required to perform their specific duties and are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality.
  • Legal Obligation and Protection: We reserve the right to disclose information when necessary to comply with a binding legal process, governmental investigation, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BizConnect, our users, or the public.

5. Data Security

We employ commercially acceptable physical, electronic, and managerial procedures, including SSL/TLS encryption and firewalls. However, we cannot issue an absolute guarantee that data transmitted will be 100% immune from intrusion.

6. User Control and Rights

You have the Right to Access, Rectification (via your Dashboard), and Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"), subject to our legal requirements to retain certain transaction records.

7. Policy Amendments

We reserve the right to update this policy. We will notify you of material changes through prominent notices on the Site or via email prior to the changes taking effect.
