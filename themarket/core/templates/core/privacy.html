Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2, 2025

1. Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs how BizConnect collects, uses, and discloses information from users of our platform. By accessing the Site, you acknowledge and accept the terms outlined here.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect data necessary to operate the marketplace, facilitate transactions, and improve your experience.

A. Personal Data

  • Identity: Name, username, password hash, preferences.
  • Contact: Email, phone, billing & shipping addresses.
  • Financial: Tokenized payment data via third-party processors. No raw card numbers stored.
  • Messages: Buyer-seller communication logs for dispute resolution.

B. Technical Data

  • Identifiers: IP address, browser, OS, device model.
  • Usage: Pages visited, time spent, clickstreams, referral sources.

3. How We Use Your Data

Service DeliveryManage accounts, orders & transactions.
SecurityFraud detection, audits & Terms enforcement.
ImprovementDiagnose issues, optimize & build features.
SupportRespond to queries, alerts & Inbox notices.

4. Disclosure & Third-Party Sharing

We never sell or rent your data. Disclosure is limited to three scenarios:

Transactional: Buyer & seller contact details are shared only to complete an order.
Service Vendors: Verified partners (payment processors, hosting) receive minimum data under confidentiality contracts.
Legal Obligation: Disclosed when required by law or to protect BizConnect, users, or public safety.

5. Data Security

We use SSL/TLS encryption and firewalls to protect your data. No method is 100% immune from intrusion.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct (via your Dashboard), or request deletion of your data, subject to legal retention requirements.

7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy and will notify you of material changes via site notice or email before they take effect.
